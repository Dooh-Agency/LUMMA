4D E-Motion Marketing Manual

Marketing actions are important not only to maintain the captive 4D E-Motion audience but also to attract new fans and therefore, improve the occupancy.

This manual adresses the 4D E-Motion marketing guidelines in order to know its acceptable uses in various situations.

The branding core elements must be used consistently in order to mantain the brand strong and cohesive worldwide.

To effectively apply the standards manual we also suggest to work starting from the originals provided. To acquire a different special application to the ones raised, please contact our design team.

THEATER

To make visitors know the format and encourage moviegoers to experience a movie in 4D E-Motion, branding should be present in the whole way through the Mall:
- Venue spots - Theater lobby - Ticket sales kiosks - Screens

THEATER

Venue Spots
Promote 4D E-Motion in various places of the venue such as parking lots, escalators, elevators or hallways.
Please contact our design team, for customized artworks.

THEATER

Ticket Sales Kiosks
To encourage the moviegoer to select the 4D E-Motion format, we suggest adding a screen saver with a brand artwork in the ticket kiosks that includes now playing movie posters, logo and slogan

TRAILER REACTION
Influencer invites a local celebrity to experience trailers of the upcoming movies in this format. This activity is shot by a professional team and has a branding pack itself. Graphics toolkit could be provided for Instagram stories, IGTV and YouTube. Enthusiastic reactions and tags increase the followers and interest in experiencing the format.
Instagram / IGTV

MOVIES REACTION
Influencer invites a local celebrity to experience a movie in 4D E-Motion. This activity is shot with smartphone. Stories express the amazing experience that 4D E-Motion offers. Enthusiastic reactions and tags increase the followers and interest in experiencing the format.
Sample quotes: "IT'S AN AMAZING EXPERIENCE" / "YOU FEEL EVERYTHING"

INVITATIONS
Local celebrities are invited to go to a 4D E-Motion movie and post a story about it. Enthusiastic reactions and tags increase the followers and interest in experiencing the format.

TICKET GIVE AWAY
To create a fanbase, boost engagement and get new followers a contest could be run. To win tickets, the audience should: follow + like + tag + share.
